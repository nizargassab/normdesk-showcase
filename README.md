# NormDesk

A multi-company workspace combining CRM, agent workflows and document-based AI assistance. Each company has its own working context, and the assistant uses the documents available to the authenticated user.

**My role:** Freelance Full-Stack Developer  
**Author:** [Nizar Gassab](https://github.com/nizargassab)

[View project](https://normdesk.ai/) · [Portfolio case study](https://nizar-gassab-portfolio.vercel.app/projets/normdesk/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nizar-gassab-b872b5244/)

## What I worked on

### 1. Agent workspace and CRM

Designed and implemented business workflows with React, TypeScript and PostgreSQL.

### 2. Authentication and context

Integrated OTP/JWT authentication and tenant-aware access to the company workspace and its documents.

### 3. Voice and document assistance

Integrated a Vosk/Ollama voice workflow that uses authorized company knowledge to support the team.

## Technology stack

| Area | Technologies |
| --- | --- |
| Business interface | React, TypeScript |
| Data and identity | PostgreSQL, OTP, JWT |
| AI and voice | Ollama, Vosk, document-grounded assistance |
